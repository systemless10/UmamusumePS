#title Toward the Shuka Sho
0 The day of the Shuka Sho. At Kyoto, same as her debut race, Vivlos is welcomed by a huge crowd of fans.
1 At her debut they were mostly her mother's and sister's fans, but now she's surrounded by voices cheering for her. Vivlos's family came to cheer too.
2 They've done everything they could—with the Trainer's encouragement, Vivlos heads boldly into the race.
