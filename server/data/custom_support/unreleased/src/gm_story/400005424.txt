#title The Grandmasters Begins!
0 With the SWBC behind us, a new year began. The trainers held the hoofprints they'd left with their Umamusume dear.
1 Like the Umamusume and trainers of the past behind the knowledge the Three Goddesses taught us, they wished their own journey would be passed down too.
2 And then—
3 The Umamusume who'd made it through the SWBC, closest of all to the peak, received invitations to the Grandmasters.
4 The Three Goddesses, keepers of countless Umamusume's data, with running at the very peak of Umamusume—the showdown against the three of them was finally at hand...!
