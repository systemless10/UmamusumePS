#title To Those Who Run Onward
0 After the Grandmasters, today, too, the voices of Umamusume, trainers, and the Three Goddesses can be heard in the simulator—
1 The Three Goddesses, said to be the forebears of Umamusume, support the Umamusume and trainers of today through the history of those who came before.
2 So that someday the path <chrname> and I walked would be passed down and told for generations, I silently cheered her on to run with all her heart.
