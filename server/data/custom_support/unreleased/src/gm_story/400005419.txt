#title Those Who Believe
0 "I want to win with my own running"—Bitter Glasse confessed her feelings to Trainer Kashimoto. But Kashimoto couldn't easily accept them.
1 To win with her old running style, she'd need excessive training, and that comes with the risk of injury...
2 As Trainer Kashimoto agonized, Little Cocon, who'd been watching, asked her to watch her run right then—and ran an astonishing time.
3 That run, beyond all expectations, came about because Little Cocon believed not only in her own weapon, but in Kashimoto's as well.
4 Trainer Kashimoto's weapon: her thorough management, facing her Umamusume and devoting herself to them. That's what produced results beyond expectations.
5 And Little Cocon told her to believe in that management, not knowledge, for Bitter Glasse too—to believe in her own weapon.
6 Having changed her mind, Trainer Kashimoto resolved to use her management skills to make Bitter Glasse win with her old running style.
7 Byerley Turk told the story of a trainer whose management once carved an Umamusume's name into history, and gave her approval.
